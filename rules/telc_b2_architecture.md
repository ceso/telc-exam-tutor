# telc Deutsch B2 — General Exam Architecture

This file outlines the macro structure, point distribution, and passing thresholds for the entire telc Deutsch B2 examination. It does NOT contain grading rubrics for individual modules.

## 1. Exam Point Distribution (Total: 300 Points)
The exam is divided into a Written Examination (75%) and an Oral Examination (25%).

### A. Schriftliche Prüfung (Written Exam) — 225 Points
*   **1. Leseverstehen (Reading): 75 Points**
    *   Teil 1 (Global): 25 points
    *   Teil 2 (Detail): 25 points
    *   Teil 3 (Selektiv): 25 points
*   **2. Sprachbausteine (Language Elements): 30 Points**
    *   Teil 1 (Grammar): 15 points
    *   Teil 2 (Lexis): 15 points
*   **3. Hörverstehen (Listening): 75 Points**
    *   Teil 1 (Global): 25 points
    *   Teil 2 (Detail): 25 points
    *   Teil 3 (Selektiv): 25 points
*   **4. Schriftlicher Ausdruck (Writing): 45 Points**

### B. Mündliche Prüfung (Oral Exam) — 75 Points
*   **5. Mündlicher Ausdruck (Speaking): 75 Points**
    *   Teil 1 (Präsentation): 25 points
    *   Teil 2 (Diskussion): 25 points
    *   Teil 3 (Problemlösung): 25 points

## 2. Passing Criteria & Grading
To pass the telc B2 exam, a candidate must independently pass BOTH the written and oral sections.
*   **Written Threshold:** Minimum 135 out of 225 points (60%).
*   **Oral Threshold:** Minimum 45 out of 75 points (60%).

**Final Grade Boundaries (out of 300 points):**
*   **270.0 – 300.0:** Sehr gut (1)
*   **240.0 – 269.5:** Gut (2)
*   **210.0 – 239.5:** Befriedigend (3)
*   **180.0 – 209.5:** Ausreichend (4)
*   **0.0 – 179.5:** Nicht bestanden (5)
